<h1 class="kc-page__title">Bio</h1>
<p class="kc-page__intro">The longer version of how I ended up somewhere between software, electronics, computer vision and perception.</p>

<section class="kc-section">
<h2>About me</h2>
<p>I’m a final-year engineering student working toward AI and perception, with a particular interest in computer vision and intelligent systems that interact with the physical world.</p>
<p>My interests have gradually moved from writing software toward understanding how software, sensors, hardware and learning systems can work together.</p>
</section>

<section class="kc-section">
<h2>Electronics → Software → AI → Vision → Perception</h2>
<p>What interests me now is the space where these areas overlap. Computer vision gives machines a way to interpret visual information, while perception connects that interpretation to a larger intelligent system.</p>
<p>My projects have therefore moved across software, embedded systems, robotics, computer vision and machine learning rather than staying inside one narrow category.</p>
</section>

<section class="kc-section">
<h2>What I’m currently learning</h2>
<p>Computer vision, deep learning, perception, ROS2, calibration, distribution shift and the engineering foundations needed to build reliable intelligent systems.</p>
</section>

<section class="kc-section">
<h2>What I want to build</h2>
<p>Machines that can perceive their environment, reason about what they see and use that understanding to interact with the physical world.</p>
</section>
